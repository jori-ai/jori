Meet Jori,
your AI coworker.

Customer email, answered. Meetings, scheduled and prepped. Business trips, planned and booked. Invoices and spend, watched. The weekly report, written before Monday. Jori takes on your team's busywork, day and night. It's private and secure by design, so your company's data stays your company's. And it's yours to shape, until it works exactly the way your business does.

Hand it the work. Get your week back.

Give Jori a role, the way you would a new hire. It learns how your team works and takes it from there.

  • InboxCustomer and vendor email sorted, answered and followed up. The urgent ones reach you first.
  • MeetingsTimes found across calendars, agendas sent ahead, notes and next steps out the moment it ends.
  • Business travelFlights and hotels that fit your travel policy, booked, tracked and rebooked when plans change.
  • Finance opsInvoices chased, receipts filed, spend watched. Anything unusual is flagged before it's a problem.
  • Sales follow-upEvery lead gets a reply and every promise gets kept. Your CRM stays up to date on its own.
  • ReportsThe numbers your team asks for, pulled together and sent on schedule.
  • OperationsSystems, renewals and deadlines watched around the clock. You hear about it before your customers do.
