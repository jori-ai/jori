Meet Jori,
your AI coworker.

Customer email, answered. Meetings, scheduled and prepped. Business trips, planned and booked. Invoices and spend, watched. The weekly report, written before Monday. Jori takes on your team's busywork, day and night. It's private and secure by design, so your company's data stays your company's. And it's yours to shape, until it works exactly the way your business does.

Follow on GitHub Be among the first to try it.

Your company's data stays yours.

Jori runs on servers you control, in your office or your own cloud account. What it learns about your customers, deals and people never ends up in someone else's product.

Nothing hidden.

Open source, top to bottom. Every job is a plain-language playbook and every action is on the record, so you always know what it did and why.

Shaped to your business.

Teach it your processes, connect your tools and set your own rules. Jori works the way your team already does, not the other way around.

All it takes: two things you already have.

1 · Your Claude plan

The one your team already uses.

2 · A server that stays on

A machine in the office or a small cloud server.

Either way it's yours: Jori, its memory and everything it learns about your business live on a server you control and can wipe.
