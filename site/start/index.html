Meet Jori

An assistant that pays attention, even when you're not asking.

Jori remembers what matters to you, reaches out before you think to ask, and gets things done. Nothing spends without your yes. It's open source and runs on the Claude subscription you already have.

What I do, in six lines.

Each one comes with a sample of how it sounds.

  • I remember you.

    People, places, preferences and what you decided, kept as pages you can read and fix.

    JoriNoted: Jordan doesn't eat oysters, and Biscuit's walk is at 8:30. I won't ask again.

  • I reach out first.

    A brief before you're up. Quiet when nothing matters.

    Jori · 7:02 AMGood morning, Sam. Rain from 4 PM, so walk Biscuit before then. Nothing else needs you today.

  • I get things done.

    Bookings, orders and errands, start to finish. Anything that spends waits for your Face ID.

    JoriFound them: same seats as last time, $1,284 for two. Approve with Face ID and they're booked.

  • I keep watch.

    Prices, shows, birthdays and the things you'd otherwise check by hand.

    JoriThe Lisbon fare dropped $142 overnight. And Jordan's birthday is in three weeks, no gift yet.

  • I handle the inbox.

    Mail sorted and filed, with only what needs you brought to you.

    Jori14 new emails. One needs you: the plumber can come Tuesday at 9. The rest are filed.

  • I get better every week.

    Every "skip" teaches me what to leave out.

    skipGot it. Weekend picks only when the forecast is good from now on.

On the Claude subscription you already have.

  • No API key. Jori signs in with your Claude Max account, the same way Claude Code does.
  • No per-message bill. Your subscription covers it. There's no meter running in the background.
  • No surprises. If the subscription isn't available, Jori stops and tells you. It never switches to something you pay for by the message.

Where should I run?

Jori needs a computer that stays on. Use your own, or let us run one for you.

Free · Open source

Run it yourself.

On a Mac that stays on, or a Linux server you rent. You create the accounts Jori uses, and the setup page walks you through each one and tests it before saving.

Why

  • Everything works, including iMessage and Notes on a Mac.
  • Your data stays on machines and accounts you own. App notifications pass through our relay, encrypted so we can't read them.

What it takes

  • About 30–60 minutes of setup.
  • You keep the computer on.
