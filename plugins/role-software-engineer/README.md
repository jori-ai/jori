# Product (role plugin)

All the tech: builds what the CEO asks for, fixes what breaks, improves Jori and ships the apps, and keeps the machines up, backed up, safe and within budget (it took over IT & Security on 2026-11-12).

A team role (system/org.md): it owns the jobs listed under `role.jobs` in plugin.json (an installation adds its own in agent/local/config/org.json), signs what they send, and appends `specialization.md` to each of their playbooks at dispatch. Name it in org.json (`names`). Turn on: `jori plugin enable role-software-engineer`.
